01 / ABOUT US

Technology moves
when we do.

A student-led space to
turn ideas into action.

ITSA — the Information Technology Student Association — is the student community of the IT Department at Pimpri Chinchwad College of Engineering and Research, Pune. We connect classroom learning with real-world technology through hands-on experiences, peer learning, and industry interaction.

An open community for IT students at PCCOER to build, learn, and lead. From hackathons to industry talks, we create opportunities that turn curiosity into capability.
